PUBLIC SOURCE CONTACT REVIEW - STEP 629

Copy one public business profile and its HTTPS source URL into Review contacts
from public source text. Select UAE local phone formatting only for a source
known to use UAE numbers. Choose Find contact candidates, review source lines,
and select the email/phone belonging to that profile. Shared inboxes or support
numbers may appear; matching text does not establish ownership or consent.

Choose Use selected contacts in prospect form, then confirm Apply selected
contacts to form. This replaces email, phone and source, including blank selected
fields. Identity, relevance, WhatsApp and other resources remain unchanged.
Complete the profile and save explicitly. Duplicate checks still apply, including
shared emails; do not invent unique addresses to bypass duplicate checks.

Parsing happens in browser memory, without a website fetch, AI call, search credit,
clipboard monitoring or automatic database save. Clear copied source text removes
the helper preview; it does not undo details already applied to the prospect form.
Input edits clear old candidates. Up to 30,000 characters and 50 unique candidates
per contact type are supported. Names, roles and WhatsApp are never inferred.
International phone formatting is syntactic, not validation. Dates, short office
identifiers and zero are excluded. Copied source text is rendered as plain text.

Refresh only updates the dashboard; it preserves the running API, its SerpApi
configuration and daily quota. No research scheduler, marketing or calling starts.
